No. 03

Meet Dianna

"Great hair never starts with scissors or color. It starts with a conversation."

Dianna is the owner and stylist behind Salon Sculpt, and every appointment with her begins the same way — with a consultation. Before any color is mixed or a single strand is cut, she listens: what you love about your hair, what frustrates you, how much upkeep fits your real life.

From there, the plan is built around you — your texture, your tones, your routine. It's a slower, more personal way to do hair, and it's why clients trust the chair at Salon Sculpt with everything from a first balayage to a full transformation.
