our story

Two Cities. One Bakery.

Crisp Crust Bakery Coffee brings together the polish of a Paris pastry kitchen and the heart of a Caracas café. Think laminated dough folded the French way, fillings with Latin soul, and a case that changes with the bakers' mood.

Find us on Billingsley Boulevard in Midland — come for the croissants, stay for the coffee, and leave with a box for the road.

Classic French pastries, laminated doughs, and bistro-style cakes and desserts.
