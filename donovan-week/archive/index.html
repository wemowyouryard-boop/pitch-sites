How to read this page

Customer / Property — who the work was for, and where.
Work done — what was done on that visit.
Status — ✅ Finished = completed · Scheduled = coming up.
Time on site — Crew: the start/finish times reported by the crew. Phone: times captured automatically from location, kept as a cross-check.
Man-hours — the true labor in a job: time on site × men on the crew (2 men working 1 hour = 2 man-hours).
Averages — the typical minutes at each property, figured from the reported times.
