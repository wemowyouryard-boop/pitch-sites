About Claws Couture

📍 Fargo, ND — private nail booth located inside Top Knotch Salon.

Claws Couture offers professional nail services with a focus on durable extensions and custom art — from builder gel and Gel X to acrylic full sets, gel manicures and luxury pedicures. Every set is finished with precision, and design add-ons range from French tips and chrome to 3D art and gems.

Questions about an appointment? Reach Amanda at amanda.strong5@outlook.com or (701) 535-1302.

Services

Builder Gel Extensions

Long-lasting builder gel extensions for smooth, flawless nails that last — also available as a builder gel application on your natural nails for a strong, protective overlay.

Gel X Extensions

Luxurious Gel X extensions for durable, stunning nails that stand out with ease.

Builder Gel Fill

A precision fill that blends seamlessly with your existing enhancements for a flawless finish. Can fill builder gel or Gel-X.

Acrylic Full Set & Fill

A fresh acrylic full set — from shorties to extra-long — plus acrylic fills to refill and repaint and keep your set looking fresh.

Gel Manicure

Long-lasting, flawless nails with vibrant color and lasting shine without chipping.

Pedicures

Luxury pedicures featuring callus removal, scrub, nail trimming, cuticle care, and an acrylic or gel polish finish. All pedicures include a deep moisturizing foot mask.

Custom Nail Art

Full designs on every nail or single-nail designs, French tips, chrome designs, 3D art, and gems & jewellery to add a touch of elegance.

Repairs & Removals

Gel nail repair, gel polish removal with nail conditioning and hydration, extension removal, and foreign extension removal.

Moisturizing Hand Mask

Revive dry hands with a moisturizing hand mask — nourish and hydrate for silky smooth skin.
