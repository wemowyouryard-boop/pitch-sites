Our Story

A Corpus Christi institution since 1960

Some places change with every trend. City Bakery has done one thing, the same way, for more than sixty years: open early, bake fresh, and take care of the neighbors who walk through the door.

Generations of Corpus Christi families have started their mornings here on South 19th Street — with biscuits for breakfast, pan dulce for the road, and a cake or pie when there's something to celebrate. Simple, warm, and proud of every one of those six decades.
