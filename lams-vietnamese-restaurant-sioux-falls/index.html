Pho, your way — three broths

Most pho spots give you one broth. Lam's gives you a choice. Pick the style that fits your mood, then load it up with herbs, sprouts, and lime the way it should be.

Light broth

Too many restaurants treat vegan dishes as an afterthought. Lam's serves a full vegan and vegetarian menu, cooked with the same care as everything else on Rice Street.

Vegan Noodle Soup
