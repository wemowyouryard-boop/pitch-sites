About

Taki Taco Pho Restaurant & Bakery is a family-owned restaurant run by a husband-and-wife team in Springfield. It serves authentic Vietnamese cuisine — pho, broken rice and a Bami Burger — alongside bakery goods and desserts, fresh sugarcane juice and boba teas, with full vegan, vegetarian and gluten-free choices.
