About

"Hi, I'm Katy, the hands behind Leaf and Lacquer. As a nature-inspired nail technician, I bring an earthy, calming touch to every set I create. I specialize in hard gel nail enhancements that are not only durable but beautifully sculpted to complement your natural nail and personal style."

Her approach blends precision with creativity, focusing on structure, strength, and style — including unconventional, nature-inspired designs like inlaying real leaves, petals, or other organic elements into hard gel enhancements. Each appointment is a calm, one-on-one experience where your nails become tiny, wearable works of art.

Services

Structured ManicureGel ManicureSignature PedicureHard Gel OverlayHard Gel ExtensionsFull Bloom Extensions

Structured Manicure

A structured manicure using Light Elegance JimmyGel to add strength and a smooth, even finish to your natural nails — perfect for preventing chips and breaks while keeping a natural, polished look, finished with top coat or a gel polish color of your choice.

Gel Manicure

Detailed cuticle care, nail trimming and shaping, and a gel polish application in the color of your choice for a long-lasting, matte or glossy finish.

Basic Manicure (No Polish)

A rejuvenating manicure with gentle cuticle care, meticulous trimming, shaping and buffing, finished with hydrating cuticle oil — no polish.

Hard Gel Overlay

A strong, natural-looking layer of Light Elegance Lexy Hard Gel over your natural nails, adding strength and supporting healthy growth — ideal for growing out your nails without extensions — with detailed cuticle care and a gel polish finish.

Hard Gel Nail Extensions

Custom sculpted gel extensions, carefully built using forms to fit your natural nails, finished with the gel polish color of your choice for a seamless, personalized look. Full Bloom Extensions add length with 6+ accent nails for a statement look.

Pedicures & Care

Signature Pedicure — a soothing soak, nail shaping, buffing, exfoliation, massage, and a warm hot towel wrap, finished with gel or traditional polish. Also Basic Gel Pedicure, Super Basic Pedicure, Fill or Rebalance, Accent Nails, Gel Polish Removal, and Nail Repair.
