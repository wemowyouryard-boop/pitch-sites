About DreamGlow Studio LLC

Kim is the artist behind DreamGlow Studio, a manicure-only studio specializing in natural nail care. In the beauty and spa industry since 2011, her approach centers on healthy natural nail maintenance, meticulous cuticle care, intentional shaping, and structured gel support when it is the right fit. DreamGlow offers manicures exclusively — no pedicures.
