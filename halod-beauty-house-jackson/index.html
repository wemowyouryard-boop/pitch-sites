About

About the Artist My name is Brittany—licensed barber, stylist, and the hands behind Halod Beauty House. After years of working with a diverse clientele, I realized how many people were looking for more than a quick service. They wanted real guidance, cleaner techniques, healthier hair, consistent grooming, and a space where they could relax without judgment or rushed energy. Halod Beauty House was created to fill that gap! Here, the focus is on intention, wellness, and results you can see and feel.
