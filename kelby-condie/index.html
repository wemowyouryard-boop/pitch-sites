Introduction

The Man

One man, many forms — king on Monday, holy man by Friday, pirate captain whenever the sunset requires one.

Collected here is every known form of Kelby: hero, sage, scoundrel, saint, and sculpture — assembled by his best friend, who has seen it all and can confirm that every word of the legend is earned. The beard is epic, the handlebar mustache a landmark, and the man himself simply one of one.

The Collection

The Portraits

Twenty-four portraits of a single sitter — kings, sages, heroes and scoundrels, all unmistakably Kelby.

The Inner Life

Passions

No. 01Star Trek

A devoted Trekkie with pure Starfleet energy. Boldly going — everywhere, always, beard first. Live long and prosper: he fully intends to.

No. 02Collectibles

A serious collector. If it exists in miniature, clay, copper, or plastic brick form, there is a strong chance it already has a place of honor in his collection.

No. 03Astrology & Spirit

Deeply into astrology and spiritualism. He reads the stars like a star chart — because to Kelby, that is exactly what they are.

No. 04Meditation

A deep meditator. Jungle shrines, cosmic portraits, total stillness — he can quiet his mind in any dimension, including this one.

No. 05Self-Mastery

Monk-level self-discipline and iron willpower, maintained as part of his spiritual practice. Unbothered. Unshaken. Unbreakable.

A Moment of Reverence

An Ode to the Beard

Exhibit AThe Beard

Scholars have measured it. Poets have attempted it. lesser beards have simply gone home. Kelby's beard is not facial hair — it is a geographic feature, a rolling landscape of legend that has been photographed from space (the space in every room he enters).

It has survived jungles, oceans, oil painters, and being sculpted in clay twice. It fears nothing. It conditions itself on pure confidence.

Exhibit BThe Mustache

The handlebar mustache is the beard's crown jewel — curled to a perfection that traffic engineers study and bullfighters respect. Legend holds that it once directed traffic during a blackout. The legend has not been corrected, because nobody present disagreed.

“It's not a beard. It's a landmark.” — Everyone who has seen it
