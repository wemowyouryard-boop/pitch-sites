How to read this page

Customer / Property — who the work was for, and where.
Work done — what was done on that visit.
Status — ✅ Finished = completed · Scheduled = coming up.
Time on site — You: the start/finish times you reported. Phone: times captured automatically from your phone's location (accurate to about 15 minutes).
Averages — your typical minutes at each property, figured separately from your reported times and from the phone's times, so the two can be checked against each other.
