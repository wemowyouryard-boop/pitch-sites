A locally owned café & gift shop

Atticus Coffee & Gifts is a locally owned café, coffee shop, and gift shop in Spokane, Washington.

Under one roof you will find a café serving espresso, pastries, and sandwiches, bulk coffee and tea, and a gift shop with books, candles, soaps, cards, and shirts.
