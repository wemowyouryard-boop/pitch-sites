A Salinas morning tradition

Peninsula Pastries is part of the morning rhythm on South Main Street in Salinas — the kind of bakery where the day starts with the smell of butter, fruit and warm bread.

The cases fill early with croissants, pastries, cakes, pies and cookies, and when they're gone, they're gone. That's the way a neighborhood bakery should be — small, fresh, and worth getting up for.
