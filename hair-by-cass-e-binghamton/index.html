About

Hi I’m Cassie! My top priority as a stylist is maintaining the health of your hair & scalp while helping you achieve your hair goals. We all know it’s hard to take time out of the day for ourselves and self care is one of the most important things you can do. I want to make sure that everyone leaves with a smile and has a positive and comforting experience during the time we have together! I specialize in lived in colors, hand tied extensions, vivid’s and bridal hair styling.
