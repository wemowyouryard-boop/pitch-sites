About

Hi, I’m Chrissy 🤍 I’m the owner and stylist behind Wild Ivy Salon, a private studio in Modesto, California. I’ve been behind the chair for over 13 years, specializing in dimensional color, gray coverage, and extensions. My goal is simple: beautiful, wearable hair that feels like you. Every appointment is customized around your hair, lifestyle, and long-term goals — whether you’re here for your regular root refresh or a complete transformation. I’m certified in multiple extension methods and continue to invest in advanced education so I can offer techniques that prioritize both beautiful results and the health of your natural hair.
