How these numbers were figured

The ranges come from 2026 cost data for this kind of work: national bathroom remodel cost bands by project scope (HomeDesigns.ai 2026 data), Oklahoma City project examples published by Thumbtack, and the Journal of Light Construction Cost vs. Value report for Oklahoma City (a midrange bath remodel's job cost there is about $24,800). Your answers about size, scope, and finish level move the range up or down. It is a ballpark estimate — not a quote, and not a promise of any contractor's price.
