About

Sallese & Company is a hair salon at 709 56th Street in downtown Kenosha, founded by Chelsea Sallese, its founder and leading stylist. The menu is built around color packages, root touch-ups, glazes, and handtied extensions, plus makeup application and special-occasion styling.
