About

Hi, I'm Gwenn! owner of Glam by Gwenn LLC, your favorite local boho licensed cosmetologist, hairstylist, barber, brow artist, and makeup artist proudly serving Jacksonville, North Carolina, and the surrounding Eastern NC area. With over 10 years of experience in the beauty industry, my passion has always been helping people feel confident, beautiful, and empowered. I believe beauty isn't about changing who you are—it's about enhancing the unique beauty you already possess. My journey hasn't always been easy.
