Start with the Pineapple Express

Outdoor Seating

Dinner on Government Street

When the Gulf Coast weather turns kind, take your table outside. Our front patio puts you right on historic Government Street — order the Pineapple Express, watch the world go by, and linger a little longer over dessert.

It's one of the best seats in the neighborhood for a slow evening with family and friends.

Our Story

A family kitchen since 2017

Aroy Thai Cuisine is family-owned and family-run. Since 2017, our family has cooked the food we grew up loving for our neighbors here in Mobile — and Mobile has loved us right back.

We're proud to hold a perfect 5.0-star rating on Google, earned one plate at a time. Whether it's your first visit or your fiftieth, you'll be treated like family here — because to us, that's exactly what guests are.
