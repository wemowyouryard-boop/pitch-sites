About Saigon Tapioca

Saigon Tapioca is a family-run Vietnamese restaurant in Colorado Springs, serving pho noodle soups, spring rolls, stir-fried meats with rice and chicken wings, plus boba drinks. Rated 4.8 on ThreeBestRated. Delivery and takeout available.
