Meet the owner

Christina Santiago built a salon around one idea: renewal.

Since 2017, owner and master stylist Christina Santiago has run Revive Salon on a simple promise — nobody leaves in the same mood they walked in with. Every appointment starts with a real conversation about what you want to change, and ends with a look that feels like you, renewed.

From precision cuts and custom color to extensions and keratin treatments, the chair at Revive is where Syracuse comes to shed the old season and start the next one.

  • Owner-operated — Christina is on the floor, not behind a desk
  • Master stylist appointments available by request
  • Cut, color and styling under one roof on Erie Boulevard
