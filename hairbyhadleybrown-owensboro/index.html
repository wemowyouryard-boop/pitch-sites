About

Hi, I’m Hadley! I’m a hairstylist specializing in lived-in blondes, dimensional brunettes, and sew-in weft extensions. My goal is to create beautiful, customized hair while keeping the integrity and health of your hair a top priority. Whether you’re looking for a subtle refresh or a complete transformation, I strive to make every appointment a relaxing, personalized experience where you leave feeling confident and beautiful.
