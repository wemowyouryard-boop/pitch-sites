Tony's Old Fashioned Bakery

Baked from scratch, the way it used to be done.

Tucked along North Big Spring in Midland, Tony's is an early-morning bakery in the best old sense of the word — cases filled before the sun is fully up, heritage recipes made from scratch, and the smell of fresh bread and warm cinnamon rolls drifting out the door.

The ovens start early and the doors close at two, so come by in the morning while the day's baking is still warm.

An Old Fashioned Morning

A good bakery day starts before most folks are awake. At Tony's, the morning begins with dough rising, ovens heating, and trays of danishes, cinnamon rolls, and kolaches coming out golden — the same unhurried, from-scratch way this kitchen has always worked.

It's the kind of place where breakfast is a warm pastry and a moment to slow down, and where a box of cookies or a custom cake still gets made one at a time, by hand, for the person who ordered it.

Come early — when it's gone for the day, it's gone.
