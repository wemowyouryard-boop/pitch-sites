01 — The Studio

An Arts-District
Institution

Tucked into the storefronts of Elmwood Avenue, Studios at 806 has been part of the neighborhood's creative fabric for over two decades.

806 Elmwood Ave

Since , Studios at 806 has done hair the Elmwood Village way — personal, unhurried, and a little bit artful. Our chairs are home to both independent stylists and in-salon professionals, each bringing their own craft and clientele under one roof.

Regulars who've been with us for years, first-timers chasing a new look — the studio runs on the mix. Come as you are; leave as the version of yourself you've been picturing.
