01 — The Room

Sophistication,
poured nightly.

Step into an oasis of sophistication and elegance, where every detail has been carefully curated to offer an unparalleled experience of opulence.

Oak Room Bar and Lounge is an exclusive, high-end retreat in Midtown Oklahoma City, designed for those who seek the finest in style — marble tables under warm brass light, the gold crest glowing over the room, and a crowd that dresses for the night.

Come for karaoke, stay for the DJs. From live performances to R&B Sundays, every night of the week has its own signature.
