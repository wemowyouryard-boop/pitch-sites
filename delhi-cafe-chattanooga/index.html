From Our Kitchen

Cooked the Delhi way — bold, fresh, and made to share

Every plate starts with whole spices, slow cooking, and bread baked to order in the clay tandoor oven.

Curries & Biryani

Rich, slow-simmered curries and fragrant basmati biryani layered with spice — the heart of the menu, in chicken, lamb, and vegetable versions.

Tandoori & Fresh Breads

Meats marinated in yogurt and spice, roasted blistering-hot in the tandoor — served with naan and breads puffed fresh for every order.

Vegan-Friendly Plates

Plant-based chickpea curries, lentil dals, and vegetable dishes — look for the green Vegan tag throughout the menu.

Wraps & Street Snacks

The flavors of a Delhi street corner: crisp samosas, tangy chaat, Indo-Chinese starters, and tikka wraps rolled in fresh naan.

Private Dining Room

A dedicated room for groups and events — family celebrations, office gatherings, and parties with a full Indian spread.

Dine-In & Takeout

Sit down with family in our dining room, or call ahead and we'll have your order hot and ready to take home.
