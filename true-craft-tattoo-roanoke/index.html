Tattoo & Piercing Shop

Tattooing is a craft of permanence

True Craft Tattoo is a high quality tattoo shop in historic downtown Roanoke. The goal of True Craft is to make sure each and every tattoo is done to its full and complete potential — to reflect what the customer wants, and beyond.

How to book

Walk-ins & appointments

True Craft offers walk-in availability and appointments for more involved, larger tattoos. Consultations are available to discuss your design and placement.
