About

Welcome to Essentially Flo LLC — your home for expert Loc and Natural Hair Care. I’m Flo, a licensed Natural Hair Stylist and Master Loctician specializing in locs, loc repairs, and overall natural hair health for all ages and textures. My mission is to restore, maintain, and celebrate natural hair — with a focus on strengthening roots, promoting growth, and educating clients along the way.
