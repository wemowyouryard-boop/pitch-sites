👋 About Us

This platform wasn't dreamed up in an office somewhere — it was built on a mowing route.

Justin Marks owns Giving Tree Lawn Care, LLC in Oklahoma City. He built this system to run his own weeks: four routes, dozens of customers, one searchable record of every visit, and the real minutes every property takes.

It worked so well for his own business that he now offers it to other lawn care owners through his website service — the same setup, branded for your business, run for you every week.
