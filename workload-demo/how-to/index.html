❓ How to Use

No app to learn, no logins to remember. If you can send a text, you can run this.

  1. We set up your week. Send us your customers and routes once — we build your week page, your archive, and your customer list.
  2. Work your week like always. Your week page shows each day's stops. Finished a yard? Just tell us — or tap check-in on your phone — and the stop is marked finished on your page the same day.
  3. Times log themselves. Your phone's location can stamp when you arrive at and leave each property. You can also give us times yourself ("Johnson, 7:31 to 8:02") — both are kept, side by side, so the numbers check each other.
  4. Look back anytime. Your archive holds every week. Search any customer or street and see every visit, how long it took, and what was done.
  5. Billing takes minutes. Invoices build from the finished work. Review them, send them, and watch the total due on the 1st — one number, always current.

What if something changes mid-week?

Rain delay, a skipped yard, a new customer — just say the word and the week updates. The rain watch on your page flags risky days by itself, straight from the live forecast.
