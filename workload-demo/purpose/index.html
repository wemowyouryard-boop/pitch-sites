🎯 What It's For

Built for owners who work in the truck, not behind a desk.

Never lose track of a yard again

Every stop, every week, in one place — who got done, who got skipped, and what's still out there. No more reconstructing a week from texts and memory.

Know what every property really costs you

Minutes are money. The platform tracks how long each property actually takes — down to the minute — so when a yard quietly eats 55 minutes for a 30-minute price, you see it and can fix the price instead of eating it all season.

Proof of work, forever

A customer says you missed a week? Your archive has the date, the time you arrived, and how long you were there. Disputes end fast.

Billing without the Sunday-night paperwork

Invoices grow out of the finished work itself, and the total due on the 1st is always one glance away.
