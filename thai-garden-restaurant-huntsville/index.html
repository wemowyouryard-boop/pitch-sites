Welcome to Thai Garden

Huntsville’s table for real Thai cooking

Tucked on Wellman Avenue in northeast Huntsville, Thai Garden Restaurant serves the dishes Thai families actually eat — punchy, herb-driven, and balanced between hot, sour, salty, and sweet.

Join us for a quick weekday lunch or settle in for dinner. Either way, you’ll be greeted like a regular — because most of our guests are.
