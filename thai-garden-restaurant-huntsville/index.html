Our Story

The first taste of Thailand in the Rocket City

When Thai Garden opened in 1991, it was the first Thai restaurant Huntsville had ever had. Generations of families have grown up around these tables since — first dates, birthdays, and weeknight takeout — all drawn by the same thing: Thai cooking done properly, from scratch, every service.

The kitchen still works the traditional way. Curries are built on fresh paste and coconut milk, noodles are tossed to order in a hot wok, and the heat is honest — tell us how you like it, from mild to Thai hot.

Signature Dishes

The Classic

Pad Thai

Thailand's beloved stir-fried rice noodles — tamarind, egg, bean sprouts, and crushed peanut, tossed to order in the wok.

Curry

Green Curry Chicken

Fragrant green curry in coconut milk with chicken, Thai basil, and bamboo — rich, aromatic, and as spicy as you like it.

Spicy Fried Rice

Jasmine rice wok-fired with chili, garlic, and Thai basil — smoky, bold, and built for heat lovers.
