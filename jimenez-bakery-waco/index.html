Pastel de tres leches

Tres Leches Cake

A light, tender sponge cake soaked in three milks — evaporated milk, sweetened condensed milk and cream — until every bite is rich and moist, then finished with a smooth whipped topping. It's the classic celebration cake of the panadería, and it gets its own place of honor in ours.

Nuestra Historia

A Waco Tradition

Jimenez Bakery does one thing the traditional way: baking real pan dulce, fresh every morning, for the families of Waco. The recipes, the trays, the smell of sweet bread when you walk through the door — some things don't need changing.

Whether you're picking up a box of pan dulce for the weekend, cookies for the kids, or a tres leches cake for a celebration, this is the place Waco has come to for Mexican sweet bread .
