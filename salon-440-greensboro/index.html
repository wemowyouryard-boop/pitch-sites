About

As an artist, creativity is at the heart of everything I do. I’m inspired by color, texture, movement, and individuality, and I bring that artistic eye into every service. My goal is not just to create beautiful hair, but to create an experience where you feel comfortable, confident, and truly cared for. I specialize in blowouts, updos, and balayage, with a focus on creating effortless, polished looks that make my clients feel confident and beautiful.
