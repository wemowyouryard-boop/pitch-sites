About

Classy Image Hair Salon is led by Annette Ashe, a licensed cosmetologist specializing in Healthy Hair First. She works with clients with hair loss and offers hair loss treatments, wig installs, sew-ins, and wigs sold on site, alongside color, braiding, and scalp care.
