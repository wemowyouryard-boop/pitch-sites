About Brooklyn

"Hey hey! My name is Brooklyn and I am a nail technician! Creativity has always been my passion, so when I discovered the world of nail art in sixth grade, I snatched my mom's bag of nail polish — and now I've been a licensed nail technician for just over 3 years."

Brooklyn Harris is the owner and nail technician behind Midnight Nails. She specializes in gel services, from natural gel manicures to gel extensions, and especially enjoys fun nail art with sparkles, rhinestones and charms. Her goal, in the studio's own words, is to provide beautiful nails that are suitable for your lifestyle.

Services

Gel Services

  • Structured Gel Manicure — a builder gel (soft gel) builds up the natural nail and provides a layer of protection stronger than gel polish; gel polish can be applied over the builder gel. It does not add length.
  • Gel-X Full Set — pre-shaped full-cover soft gel tips that add length to the natural nail.
  • Gel-X Fill — a fill for your existing Gel-X set.
  • Nail Care — cut, file and shape, with cuticle work; buff and shine or a nail-strengthening base coat, no polish.

Art & Removals

  • Nail art add-ons — Freehand, French Tip, Chrome, Marble, Charms, Rhinestones, and Decals/Stickers.
  • Foreign Removal — removal of work done elsewhere.
  • Gel Polish Removal, Gel-X Removal, and Structured Overlay Removal.

Art pricing varies by design and how many charms, rhinestones, or decals you choose — send a picture of the design you'd like for a more accurate price.
