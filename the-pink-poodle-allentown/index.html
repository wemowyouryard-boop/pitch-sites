Our Story

Pretty in pink since 1992

The Pink Poodle has been grooming Allentown's dogs since 1992 — over three decades of baths, bows, tidy trims and wagging tails. What started with a love of poodles (can you tell?) has grown into a full-service grooming salon and dog daycare where every pup is treated like a little celebrity.

Big or small, fluffy or sleek, first-timers or lifelong regulars — your dog will always find a warm welcome, a gentle hand and a fresh new look at The Pink Poodle.
