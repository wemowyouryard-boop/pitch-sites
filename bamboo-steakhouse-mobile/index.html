Our Story · 竹 Bamboo

A Mobile tradition at the grill since 2010.

Founded in 2010 by owner John Nguyen, Bamboo Steakhouse & Sushi Bar brings the theater of teppanyaki and the craft of a real sushi bar together under one roof on Cody Road South.

Gather around the grill while your chef sears, seasons, and serves — or settle in at the sushi bar and on the lakeside patio for a quieter night. However you come in, you leave full.

  • Hibachi cooked to order — steak, chicken, seafood and vegetables, right at your table.
  • A sushi bar of its own — fresh sashimi and signature rolls, including the Bamboo Roll.
  • Room for the whole crew — private party rooms for birthdays, teams, and celebrations.
