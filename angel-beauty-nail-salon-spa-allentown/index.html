our story

A neighborhood sanctuary on Hamilton Boulevard

Tucked along Hamilton Boulevard in Allentown, Angel Beauty has been caring for the Lehigh Valley's hands, feet, lashes, and brows . Step inside and the day softens — gentle surroundings, unhurried service, and careful attention to every detail.

Whether you come for a quiet spa pedicure, a fresh set of gel extensions, or a touch of artistry on your nails, our team treats every visit as a small ritual of rest and renewal.

Manicures

Classic and spa manicures that shape, buff, and polish your natural nails to a soft, healthy shine — finished with the color of your choosing.
