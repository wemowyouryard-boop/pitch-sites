About

My name is Ursula Chambers Franklin and I have been a Licensed cosmetologist for over 20 years and a Licensed cosmetologist instructor for 10 years. I Love all things hair but my speciality is locs and Micro Locs. My services include but are limited to natural styles, Locs, loc styles, loc Retwist, starter locs, box braids, yarn braids, silk press, deep conditioning treatments, and Micro locs. 💇🏽‍♀️ I No longer service kids under 12 years old. 💇🏽‍♀️If you wanting to get MICRO LOCS, you HAVE TO BOOK THE MICRO LOC CONSULTATION. During the consultation, I will educate you on the process, measure your hair, do some hair test on your hair to see if you are a good candidate. The consultation fee can be applied to the Micro loc price as long as you book an appointment within 30 DAYS. You will be allowed to reschedule ONE TIME, but after that you will have to start back over with a consultation. 💇🏽‍♀️Transfer MICRO LOC CLIENTS HAVE TO BOOK A CONSULTATION BEFORE BOOKING AN APPOINTMENT. 💇🏽‍♀️If you are having any symptoms of being SICK or been around anyone who has been SICK (COVID, Flu) please notify me to reschedule. 💇🏽‍♀️Please don’t bring any EXTRA PEOPLE to your appointment unless they have an appointment.
