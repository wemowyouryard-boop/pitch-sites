A North Side Bakery, Through & Through

Since 2006, Columbus Baking Company has been doing one thing the old-fashioned way: baking honest Italian bread for the neighborhood. No shortcuts, no fuss — just early mornings, hot ovens, and loaves that come out the way they should.

You'll find us in the heart of Syracuse's North Side, where the smell of fresh bread has a way of stopping people on the sidewalk. Reviewers around the city call our loaf the best in Syracuse — we just call it a day's work, done right.

Come by, say hello, and take a loaf home while it's still warm.
