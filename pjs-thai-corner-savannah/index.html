Cooked by Chef Anna,
the way it should be.

PJ's Thai Corner has been a downtown Savannah fixture since 2009 — a small corner kitchen on Abercorn Street where Chef Anna cooks the food she knows best. No shortcuts, no freezer-to-fryer fare: sauces built in-house, noodles tossed to order in a screaming-hot wok, and herbs and chilies treated with respect.

It's the kind of place Savannah keeps coming back to — 4.5 stars across 125 Google reviews — for a quick lunch between the squares or a relaxed dinner under the string lights out front. Whether it's your first salmon Pad Thai or your fiftieth, Chef Anna's corner is glad you're here.

The dishes people cross town for

Two signatures anchor the menu — Chef Anna's salmon Pad Thai and her duck specials. Start there; you won't go wrong.

★ House Signature

Salmon Pad Thai

Our classic Pad Thai — rice noodles wok-tossed in tamarind sauce with egg, bean sprouts, and crushed peanut — crowned with a generous piece of salmon. The plate that built our name.
