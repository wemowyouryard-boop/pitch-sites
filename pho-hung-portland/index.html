The Menu

Order the way regulars do. Every bowl of phở comes with a plate of fresh bean sprouts, Thai basil, lime, and jalapeño on the side, so you can season it exactly the way you like it. Beyond phở, the kitchen keeps a rotating cast of other noodle soups — ask your server what’s in the pot today.

From the Vegan Kitchen

Our vegan phở is not the beef broth with the meat left out. It simmers in a separate vegan broth, made for vegetables and tofu from the start — so the bowl tastes like it was always meant to be this way.

Our House

Phở Hùng is a Vietnamese restaurant on Southeast Powell, a neighborhood favorite on Southeast Powell. It is the kind of place Portland keeps in its weekly rotation: big steaming bowls, herbs piled high, and a dining room that fills up with families, regulars, and first-timers being walked through the numbered menu by the table next to them.

Come hungry, order, and don’t be shy with the basil.

Groups & Parties

Birthdays, family gatherings, office lunches, and celebrations of every size — Phở Hùng welcomes groups of every size, so the whole table can share the spread: platters of spring rolls, bánh mì, rice plates, and bowls of phở for everyone.
