The Practice

Embodiment in Four Steps

An affirmation only works when it leaves the page and enters your body. Two minutes, every morning:

1

See It

Close your eyes and step into the scene. The light, the air, the ground under your feet — build the picture until it feels like a memory.

2

Feel It

Scan your body. Where does this version of you live — the open chest, the slow breath, the warm hands? Let the sensation get bigger.

3

Look It

Now arrange yourself like the person in the scene: spine tall, shoulders back, chin level, eyes up. Dress your posture in it.

4

Live It

Carry one small action into today — one call, one walk, one brave sentence — done as the person you just saw.

"Don't just say it. See it. Feel it. Look like it. Live it."
