About the studio

Mystic Mane Hair Studio

Every service at Mystic Mane is customized to the person in my chair. Pricing reflects the time, technique, professional products, and personalization required to create and maintain your look.

All color pricing is starting at and includes a standard product allowance. Additional product charges may apply for exceptionally long, thick, or dense hair.

Consultations are available for hair, extensions and wigs — including one-on-one time in the private suite to discuss hair piece options and find the best fit specifically for you.

Dimensional color, custom blonding, extensions & hair enhancements.

What we do

Services

Blonding Transformation

For major color changes requiring significantly more time, product, formulation, or corrective work than a traditional highlight appointment — dramatic blonde transformations and major color corrections.

Signature Full Blonding

The most complete traditional blonding service: customized highlights throughout the entire head, with professional glaze/toner, shampoo + conditioning treatment and a finished style.

Signature Partial Blonding

Dimensional blonding focused through the crown, sides, and areas where brightness makes the biggest impact — perfect for maintaining highlights or a naturally sun-kissed result.

Face Frame / Mini Blonding

The perfect refresh between larger blonding appointments — customized brightness around the face and through the most visible areas of the hair.

Custom Color

A personalized color journey with expert techniques and professional products tailored to your unique style and hair needs.

Root Color + Dimensional Blonding

A complete maintenance service for clients who need both root color and refreshed highlights — ideal for gray coverage combined with brightness and dimension.

Root Retouch & Root Color + Gloss

Maintain your existing color by refreshing regrowth at the scalp, with the option to restore shine and tone through the mids and ends.

Gloss + Toner Refresh

Bring dull, faded, brassy, or lackluster color back to life — neutralize unwanted warmth, refresh faded color, enhance richness and restore shine. This service does not include lightening.

Maintenance & Transformation Haircuts

Maintain your existing shape with a precision haircut, or choose a transformation cut for significant changes in length, shape, layering, or overall style. Bang trims and short hair / men's cuts also available.

Extensions & Enhancements

Extension consultations, extension maintenance, extension shampoo/blow dry, topper tightening, and wig consultations and wig cuts.

K18 Treatment

The transformative K18 Treatment for stronger, healthier hair from within — for vibrant, resilient locks.

Shampoo Style & Beard Trim

A soothing shampoo and style treatment with hot tool styling, plus beard trims to finish the look.
