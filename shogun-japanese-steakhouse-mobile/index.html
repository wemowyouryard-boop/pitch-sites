Hibachi · Teppanyaki

The chef-show experience

At Shogun, the grill is center stage. Your chef cooks your meal on the teppanyaki grill at your table — searing, seasoning, and serving it up with the kind of showmanship that turns an ordinary dinner into the part of the night everyone talks about.

  • Cooked before your eyes

    Hibachi steak, shrimp, and chicken hit the hot grill and come off it sizzling — no waiting on a kitchen door.

  • A table made for groups

    Birthdays, family nights, and celebrations land naturally around the grill — everyone faces the show.

  • Fried rice & noodles, grill-fresh

    Hibachi plates come together on the grill with fried rice or noodles and vegetables, seasoned to order.

From the Sushi Bar

The Kiss of Fire Roll

Every sushi bar has a signature. Ours is the Kiss of Fire Roll — the roll regulars order by name and first-timers get talked into by the table next to them. It leads a sushi and sashimi lineup rolled and sliced fresh to order, all evening long.

Good to Know

Taken care of, every visit

Allergies handled with care

Guests regularly praise our staff for how carefully they handle shellfish allergies. Tell us about any allergy when you're seated or when you call in your order, and the team will take it from there — it's the kind of attention we believe a neighborhood restaurant owes its guests.

Takeout & delivery

The full Shogun experience travels. Call ahead for takeout, or have your hibachi and sushi favorites delivered to your door.

Full bar

Pair dinner with a drink from our full bar — a natural fit for a night around the grill or a seat at the sushi bar.

Kids menu

Little chefs are welcome at the grill. Our kids menu keeps younger guests happy while the show goes on around them.

Vegan options

Plant-based guests aren't an afterthought — ask about our vegan options when you sit down or call in.
