Juliet's Pick #1

Chai Spice Dream

A cozy, naturally sweet smoothie with warm chai spices, creamy banana, and the rich caramel note of dates. Perfect for cool mornings or an afternoon reset.

Ingredients (serves 1)

  • ½ cup strong chai-spice milk tea, cooled
  • 1 ripe banana
  • ½ cup unsweetened almond milk
  • ½ teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 3 pitted dates

Method

  1. Add all ingredients to a blender.
  2. Blend on high until completely smooth and creamy.
  3. Pour into a tall glass and serve immediately.

Optional upgrades

  • Add ½ scoop vanilla protein powder for extra staying power
  • Blend in 1 tablespoon oats or chia seeds for a heartier texture
  • Sprinkle a pinch of cinnamon or nutmeg on top
