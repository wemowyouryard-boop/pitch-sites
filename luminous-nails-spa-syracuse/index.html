Welcome

A softer pace for hands & feet

Step in off West Genesee Street and slow down. At Luminous Nails & Spa, every appointment begins with unhurried attention — shaping, soaking, smoothing — and ends with nails that feel as good as they look.

Whether you come for a classic manicure, a long-wearing gel finish, or a set of designs that are entirely your own, our chairs are ready when you are. Walk-ins are welcome, and appointments are just a phone call away.

Our Services

The service menu

From everyday polish to full sculpted sets — each service is finished with care, down to the last detail.

  • 01

    Manicures

    Nail shaping, cuticle care, a relaxing hand massage and your choice of polish — the classic, done beautifully.

  • 02

    Pedicures

    A warm soak, gentle exfoliation, callus care and massage, finished with perfectly polished toes.

  • 03

    Gel Manicures

    High-shine gel color cured to a glossy, chip-resistant finish that keeps its glow for weeks.

  • 04

    Dip Powder

    A lightweight, durable alternative to traditional polish — strong, natural-feeling nails in rich, lasting color.

  • 05

    Acrylic Nails

    Sculpted length and strength, shaped to your style — from soft and natural to bold and dramatic.

  • 06

    Nail Designs

    Hand-painted art, glitter, ombré and seasonal designs. Bring a photo of what you love, or let us dream something up with you.
