The Many Lives of Jason

Sixteen years is a long time. Long enough for a man to live several lives — and somehow, every single one of them involves a yard.

Life № 1 — The Gnome Era

Guardian of the Garden

Before the trucks, before the trailers — there was the garden. Jason has been watching over Oklahoma flower beds since roughly the dawn of time. The flowers respect him. The weeds fear him.

Life № 2 — The Mowing Years

The Lawn Whisperer

They say if you listen closely on a Saturday morning, you can hear him coming. Sixteen years of stripes so straight they'd make a ruler jealous. The lawn has never won. Not once.

Life № 3 — The Championship Run

Weed-Pulling Champion

Another trophy raised high. Botanists estimate Jason has personally evicted more weeds than most small nations. He doesn't pull weeds — he serves them notice.

Life № 4 — The Space Program

Mowing the Final Frontier

When NASA needed someone who would never, ever give up — not even in a magical forest with a mower the size of a shoebox — there was only one name on the list.

Life № 5 — The Legend Today

The Man with the Golden Shears

Present day. The crepe myrtles bloom a little brighter when he shows up — partly out of admiration, partly out of respect for the shears. Sixteen years in, and just getting warmed up.

How He Operates

The Code

Every legend runs on a code. Jason's has four rules, and in sixteen years he has never broken one of them.

🤝

Loyalty

Sixteen years with the same family. In a world of job-hoppers, Jason is a landmark — you don't visit Oklahoma City without driving past him.

🔋

Endurance

August heat, frozen mornings, the yard that never ends — Jason outlasts all of it. The energizer has nothing on him.

Career give-ups: zero. Sixteen years, thousands of yards, one unbroken streak. Some records are just never meant to be broken.
