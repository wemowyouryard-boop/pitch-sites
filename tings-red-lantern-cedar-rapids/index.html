Our Story

Two seatings a day, one standard since 2004

Ting's Red Lantern has been serving Cedar Rapids since 2004 — a neighborhood Chinese restaurant on Boyson Road where the wok stays hot, the portions stay generous, and familiar dishes are made with care.

Join us for lunch, or come back in the evening when the lanterns glow for dinner. Either way, you'll find the Szechuan and Hunan specialties our regulars drive across town for.
