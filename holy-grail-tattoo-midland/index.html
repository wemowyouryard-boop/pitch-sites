A Specialty of the House

Memorial & Portrait Work, Done with Reverence

Some tattoos mark a moment. These mark a person. Holy Grail Tattoo takes on memorial and portrait pieces with the patience they deserve — careful likeness work, soft black-and-grey shading, and designs that weave in the symbols, dates, and details that tell their story.

Whether it's a portrait of someone you've lost or a tribute to someone still beside you, bring your photos and your memories. The studio will help you turn them into something you can carry always.
