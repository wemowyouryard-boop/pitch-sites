About the Studio

Capital A Creative Studio Co. sits at 1400 High St in Eugene. The work is shear, clipper, and razor — fades, beard work, and design, plus the slower services: hot towel shaves and the full executive facial care session. It is a barber's studio first, and every service on the menu reflects that.

Services

From the studio's own service menu — what each appointment actually includes.

Haircut

A combined shear/clipper/razor haircut at your desired length — anywhere from short to a longer natural fade, and styled.

Haircut + Beard

The full cut plus beard work in one appointment.

Beard

Beard trim finished with a straight razor and beard oil to keep a healthy beard looking its best.
