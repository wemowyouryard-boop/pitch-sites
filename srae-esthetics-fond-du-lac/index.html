About

SRae.esthetics LLC is run by Samantha Rae, a licensed esthetician and body sculpt practitioner in Fond du Lac. The studio offers body sculpting for the full body, abdomen, arms, legs, and neck/chin/jawline, plus lash extension sets and fills, lash lift and tint, dermaplaning, and microneedling facials.
