About

Hybrid Art Studios is a custom tattoo studio in East Peoria, open since 2009 and owned by artist Dave Ferguson. The studio takes on custom work, sleeves and large pieces across styles from realism and portrait work to black and grey and geometric — walk-ins welcome, clients 18 and over only.
