The Salon

Grooming, treated as a small ceremony

Tucked into northwest Portland, Elegant Dog Grooming LLC keeps the experience simple and serene: a clean, bright salon, gentle handling, and groomers who take the time each pet needs. No rushing, no assembly line — just careful, attentive care from bath to final brush-out.

Whether your companion needs a complete full-service groom, a tidy-up between visits, or simply a warm bath in our self-service stations, there is a quiet corner of the salon waiting for them.

  • All breeds and sizes of dogs welcome
  • Gentle handling for every dog
  • Appointments, so every pet gets unhurried attention
