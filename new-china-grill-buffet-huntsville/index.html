Three Ways to Fill Your Plate

The Buffet, the Grill & the Sushi Bar

Three stations to explore — the buffet line, the hibachi grill and the sushi bar — all under one roof on Jordan Lane, where we've been serving Huntsville since 2002.

The Buffet Line

A long line of Chinese favorites — house plates, appetizers, soups and more. Load up, sit down, and head back whenever you're ready for round two.

Hibachi Grill Station

Hot off the flat-top — the hibachi grill station is a favorite stop between trips down the buffet line, with meats and vegetables grilled up fresh.

The Sushi Bar

Rolls and sushi from our sushi bar — add a few pieces to your plate between trips to the buffet line.
