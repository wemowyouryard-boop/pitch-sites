What we do

Everything your dog needs, head to tail

Book a full groom or just the essentials — every service is handled with patience and a gentle touch.

Bathing

A thorough, warm bath that leaves the coat clean, soft, and smelling fresh — the foundation of every good groom.

Haircuts

Neat, comfortable cuts shaped to your dog — from a tidy trim to a full style, finished so the coat sits just right.

Ear Cleaning

Gentle ear cleaning as part of a healthy grooming routine, so ears stay clean, comfortable, and checked over.

Nail Trimming

Careful nail trims that keep paws comfortable and steps easy — no wrestling, just a calm and steady hand.

Paw Treatments

A little extra care for the feet that carry them everywhere — paws tidied, checked, and pampered.

Teeth Cleaning

Fresher breath and a cleaner smile — teeth cleaning that rounds out the full head-to-tail groom.

Personalized grooming, every visit

No two dogs are alike, so no two grooms should be either. Tell us what your dog likes, what makes them nervous, and how you want them to look — and we'll tailor the visit to them.
