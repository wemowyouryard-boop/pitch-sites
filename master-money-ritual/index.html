Money Ritual

No candles, no tools — only your feelings and your voice.

Before You Begin

Activating the Talisman

Simply looking at the talisman — or practising the scan — does not activate it. You cannot activate it by accident.

The talisman is activated when you perform the ritual deliberately: scanning it within the ritual, holding the feeling of pleasure while the white of the sigil turns to yellow-orange fire.

That deliberate performance is the activation. There is no separate activation ceremony.

“Only when you come to the ritual and deliberately activate it, will the effects begin.”

Before You Begin

The 11 Days

The first time, perform the ritual once a day for 11 days.

Eleven days in a row is ideal — but if you miss a day, you do not start over. Keep gaps to no more than 2 days, and finish all 11 within one month.

After the first run, repeat for 3 days in a row every 3 to 4 months. If money is flowing steadily, the 3-day version once a year is enough.

Step One

Contemplate Lack

Bring to mind a memory of lack — the feeling of life without enough money.

Feel the feeling itself, not the money: the hunger, not the lost money; the cold house, not the bank balance.

Sit with it for a while. No judgment. No blame.

The same memory each day, or a different one, is fine.

Step Two · Speak Aloud
EE-AH TZ-VAH-OTT ACH-AT-REE-ELL YOT-ZAF-CHEE-RON MET-A-TRON

Speak these words aloud, with calm authority and a clear voice. If you need privacy, imagine saying them.

The CH is as in “loch”; a K sound is fine. Pronunciation does not have to be perfect. These words call angelic aid for relief from lack, and the series ends with Metatron, binding the sigil to the power of this mighty archangel.

Step Three

Transmute to Pleasure

Now invent a pleasure — do not use a memory.

Picture something that is only possible with more money, and feel it as happening NOW: driving the car now, not hoping for it.

If it is freedom from debt, see the statement at 0, the bills stamped PAID — and feel the freedom.

Only pleasures that money makes possible. Pleasant things that need no money are left aside.

Step Four · The Talisman

Hold the pleasure feeling — not the pictures in your mind.

Keep your eyes relaxed. Never read or translate the letters; let their shapes sink in. As you scan, see the white of the image become yellow-orange fire.

The Scan
1

The Left Vertical Word

Begin at the bottom of the vertical word on the left, and scan your eyes upward to the top.

No need to turn the screen — simply scan up the word. Hold the pleasure feeling; white becomes fire.

The Scan
2

The Right Vertical Word

Move your eyes across to the vertical word on the right, and scan downward — from the top to the final letter at the bottom.

Hold the pleasure feeling; white becomes fire.

The Scan
3

The Line

Start on the right and scan across the line of letters, right to left, to the far end of the line.

Hold the pleasure feeling; white becomes fire.

The Scan
4

The Six Lines

Scan the first line from right to left. Move down one line at a time, each line right to left, until you have scanned all six lines of this section.

Hold the pleasure feeling; white becomes fire.

The Scan
5

The Bottom Words

Move to the far right, at the bottom of the image, and scan the letters of these five words, from right to left.

Hold the pleasure feeling; white becomes fire.

The Scan
6

The Outer White Circle

Scan your eyes anticlockwise around the outer white circle, three times.

Begin at the top-right — the larger gap in the words marks the start. Hold the pleasure feeling; white becomes fire.

The Scan
7

The Next Circle

Move to the next circle inward, and scan anticlockwise around it, three times.

Hold the pleasure feeling; white becomes fire.

The Scan
8

The Next Inner Circle

Move inward once more, and scan anticlockwise around this circle, three times.

Hold the pleasure feeling; white becomes fire.

The Scan
9

The Central Circle

Scan the three words of the central circle from top to bottom, each word from right to left.

Then take in the whole central circle.

Hold the pleasure feeling; white becomes fire.

The Scan
10

The Final Scan

Take in the whole sigil, scanning from the centre-top down to the base.

No more than five seconds — it is a scan, not a study.

Step Five

Transmute to Gratitude

Let the pleasure deepen and turn into gratitude.

Gratitude for the experiences themselves — not for money.

Rest in that feeling.

Step Six · Speak Aloud
EE-AH TZ-VAH-OTT ACH-AT-REE-ELL YOT-ZAF-CHEE-RON MET-A-TRON

Speak the same five words again — this time feeling a surge of gratitude as you say them.

Step Seven

Light From The Dark

Turn your attention to the inside of your chest, and imagine a vast black space there — as vast as the universe.

Let a white haze arise within it, growing brighter until it becomes glaring white light. This usually takes less than a minute.

Then let the light pour out of you — from your chest, your fingertips, your eyes — and into the talisman. Your emotions are neutral now; only the light moves.

While you are still learning, this step is optional — the ritual works without it.

Step Eight · Close
It is done.

You may say the words aloud: “It is done.”

Then close the book, and distract yourself with something ordinary and non-magickal — cook, talk, read.

Forget the magick.
