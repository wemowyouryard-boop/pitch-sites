Our story

A neighborhood pho joint with a name it has to live up to.

Serving Colorado Springs since 2012 from Stetson Hills Boulevard, Pho-nomenal does the classics properly — fragrant pho, fresh and fried spring rolls, bun vermicelli bowls and combo rice plates — with options for kids and vegan diners, too.
