An old-school Sacramento tavern

Not fast food. Real pizza, the slow way.

Zelda’s is a pizzeria and Italian restaurant built around one idea: a proper Chicago deep dish — baked in a pan, piled high, and brought to the table bubbling. Around it, a full Italian kitchen and a cozy, dimly lit dining room made for lingering.

  • Chicago Deep DishOur signature pan pizza — thick crust, generous layers, sauce on top.
  • Thin & Specialty PiesPrefer it crisp? Thin and specialty pizzas in four sizes.
  • Full Italian KitchenPastas, sandwiches, salads, and desserts beyond the pizza menu.
  • Everyone at the tableA dedicated vegan-friendly menu, plus handcrafted cocktails from the bar.
