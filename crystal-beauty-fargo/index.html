Services

Everything your nails need

Six specialties, one pair of hands. Every service is done start to finish by Diane in her private studio suite.

— 01

Builder Gel Nails

Strength and structure built right onto the nail — a hard-wearing overlay that supports length, resists lifting, and grows out beautifully.

— 02

Acrylic Nails

Classic, sculpted acrylics shaped to your hands — from a clean natural almond to full glamorous length, finished with a glass-like shine.

— 03

Gel Nails

High-gloss gel color that cures hard and stays chip-free for weeks — an enormous palette, from icy neutrals to deep jewel tones.

— 04

Custom Nail Art

Hand-painted detail made just for you — French tips with a twist, celestial accents, lacework, seasonal sets, and one-of-a-kind designs.

— 05

Pedicures with Stone Massage

A proper reset for tired feet — meticulous nail and cuticle care, a soothing warm stone massage, and a flawless polish finish.

— 06

Dip Powder on Natural Nails

Lightweight, durable color dipped onto your own nails — no lamp, no tips, just a smooth, strong finish that lets your natural nails thrive.
