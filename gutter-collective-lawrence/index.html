About

In its own words, Gutter Collective is a safe space where all your weirdest hair dreams can be procured. The salon specializes in alternative and curly hair, including shags, mullets, razor cuts, and vivid colors, and is comprised of two best friends from cosmetology school — Kit Griffin and Neo Mitchell.
