About

Welcome to Nail Church! Structured gel manicures and immaculate cuticle work are our holy rites. We also bless your skin with fully customized facials made to revive, renew and resurrect your glow. Nail Church is a sanctuary for the marginalized, the unseen, and the beautifully defiant. Women, queer folks, immigrants and anyone who’s ever been pushed to the edge: you are safe here. Hate, bigotry or demeaning talk toward any community is not tolerated. This space was built with love, and we protect it.
